A small team that plans a lot of holidays

We started because too many people came back from expensive trips slightly disappointed. Usually not because of one big failure — because of a dozen small things nobody thought about.

Our story

Built on the trips that went wrong

Unique Holidays began at a kitchen table, planning a friend's honeymoon after an online booking left them stranded at an airport with a voucher nobody would honour.

That trip got fixed. Then the next one, and the one after. Eleven years later we're a full travel desk, but the working method hasn't changed: understand how someone actually likes to travel, then build the days around that instead of around what's easy to sell.

  • Itineraries paced for real people, not for brochure photos
  • Hotels we've stayed in or inspected — never a surprise on arrival
  • One coordinator who knows your booking from enquiry to homecoming
  • Written costs and cancellation terms before a rupee changes hands

How we work

Four things we won't compromise on

We quote the real number

The figure in your first email is the figure you pay. No “taxes extra” three days before departure.

We say when not to go

If your dates are wrong for a destination, we'll tell you and suggest better ones — even if it costs us the booking.

We answer the phone

Trips go sideways at 2am. Someone from our team picks up, in your language, and fixes it.

We travel ourselves

Nobody sells a hotel here they haven't walked through or a route they haven't driven.

The team

People you'll actually talk to

No call centre and no ticket numbers. You get one planner, their direct line, and a coordinator who stays with your trip from start to finish.
